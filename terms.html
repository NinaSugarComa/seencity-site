How Seen City works

  • Your progress is the one you set. The app hides comments located after the moment you choose. It cannot check what you have actually watched: it is up to you to set it honestly.
  • When you don't know the exact minute (for example in front of live television), you can choose the “Approximate” or “Live” mode instead of “Precise”. The app then adds a safety margin: you read a little less far than your estimate, and what you write only appears for others a little later. This margin reduces the risk of spoilers without removing it.
  • Comments must be placed at the right moment. Post your comment at the moment it is about; you can then correct the text and the moment of your comments. Deliberately placing a reveal too early to spoil others' experience is forbidden.
  • Episode lengths come from TMDB. When they are missing or incomplete, users can propose a correction, which remains subject to review.
  • The Spoiler Corner is a free discussion space, open to people who have finished the episode, the season or the film (or marked it “seen”). You can talk about everything that has happened up to that point, including the ending, but not about what comes next. When you write in a Spoiler Corner older than your progress, you confirm that your message does not reveal anything about what comes next.
  • Recommendations let you suggest a title to your friends: they receive a notification, with no free text. You can turn these notifications off in “My account”.
  • You can rate out of 10 the titles you have marked “seen”. Your rating is personal: nobody else sees it. From 10 ratings on the same title, an anonymous community average is shown; it is always kept separate from the TMDB rating, which comes from TMDB.
  • You can hide a title from your friends: it then appears neither in their feed nor on your profile. This does not remove the comments you have already posted, which remain readable by people who have reached the moment concerned.
  • The daily games are free and offer no prizes: one try per game per day, the same questions for everyone. Your answers are not recorded, only your score, which stays private until you share it. It is forbidden to get around the one-try-per-day limit or to use automated tools to play. Photos and posters come from TMDB and belong to their owners. A game may be unavailable or contain an error: they are provided as is.
  • Statistics, rankings and averages are computed automatically from the app's data. They are provided for information only and depend on what people have entered in the app.
  • We do our best to prevent spoilers, but we cannot rule them out entirely.
