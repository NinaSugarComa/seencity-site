How Seen City works

  • Your progress is the one you set. The app hides comments located after the moment you choose. It cannot check what you have actually watched: it is up to you to set it honestly.
  • Comments must be placed at the right moment. Post your comment at the moment it is about. Deliberately placing a reveal too early to spoil others' experience is forbidden.
  • Episode lengths come from TMDB. When they are missing or incomplete, users can propose a correction, which remains subject to review.
  • The spoiler corner is a free discussion space, open to people who have finished the episode, the season or the film (or marked it “seen”). You can talk about everything that has happened up to that point, including the ending, but not about what comes next. When you write in a corner older than your progress, you confirm that your message does not reveal anything about what comes next.
  • Recommendations let you suggest a title to your friends: they receive a notification, with no free text. You can turn these notifications off in “My account”.
  • We do our best to prevent spoilers, but we cannot rule them out entirely.
