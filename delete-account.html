Delete my account

Last updated: November 9, 2026

You can delete your Seen City account at any time, free of charge. This page explains how, what is deleted and what may be kept.

Before you delete

Deletion is permanent: we cannot recover a deleted account. If you want to keep a copy of your data, download it first, in the app: “My account” > “Download my data”.

In the app (the fastest way)

  1. Open Seen City and sign in.
  2. Open the “My account” screen.
  3. Scroll to the very bottom and tap “Delete my account”.
  4. Confirm by tapping “Delete permanently”.

Deletion takes effect immediately in the app.

By email (if you can no longer sign in)

Write to seencity.app@gmail.com from your account's email address, with the subject “Account deletion” and your username. We delete your account and confirm it to you by email, within one month at most (usually much faster). If you write from another address, we may ask you to confirm that the account is yours.

What is deleted

  • your account (email address, username, settings) and your profile, including the cover image;
  • your comments, your Spoiler Corner messages and your reactions;
  • your progress and your lists (favorites, to watch, seen, favorite people);
  • your ratings and your hidden titles;
  • your game scores and your game history;
  • recommendations sent and received, your friends, your friend requests and your notifications;
  • the suggestions you sent to the team;
  • the stored translations of your messages.

What may be kept

  • Our hosting provider's technical backups, for a limited time, before they are erased in their normal rotation.
  • Some data, where the law requires it (for example to answer a request from an authority).
  • Anything copied or shared outside Seen City, for example a challenge sent by message: we have no way to remove it.

A question?

Write to seencity.app@gmail.com. To find out how we use your data, see the privacy policy.
